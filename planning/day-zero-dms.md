# Day Zero DMs — Captain's Log send

Paste-ready. No markdown in the message bodies: Skool DMs don't render it,
and asterisks or bracket-links show up literally. Bare URLs auto-link there.

Send each one by hand, spaced out over a few days. A hand-sent DM is the
reason the last round came back 11 for 11 — and Skool flags rapid-fire
messaging. Keep a list of who you've sent to; that list is your day 7 check.

Post the "one line from your log this week" thread BEFORE the first DM goes
out, so nobody arrives to a quiet room.

---

## Version A — the eleven who answered

```
Greetings Captain —

A while back I asked you four questions and you actually answered them. Properly. I've read that reply more times than you'd guess.

Here's what came back across all of them: almost nobody said money. It was carrying it alone, never being fully present, and losing your cool with the people you'd take a bullet for. More than one of you used the word failure about yourself.

So here's what I've got for you — it's in the Classroom, under T.H.I.S. Captain's Log:

https://www.skool.com/dad-up-dojo-1685/classroom/9f8310e9?md=eb11a4fba15d4ac883056debd65254ff

Three things you're grateful for, and an Echo — you go back to an earlier entry and ask why that happened, and what it did for the people around you. That's the whole thing. Ninety seconds.

Print it, or keep a notebook next to it. It doesn't have to be filled in on a screen.

And read this bit before you start: you are not bound by calendar days. Miss a day, miss a month. There's no streak to break and nothing to feel behind on. You're not aiming for a perfect Tuesday.

Nothing to reply to. Thank you for trusting me with the real answer — it's why this exists.

— James
```

## Why the DM asks for nothing

Day 0 gives, it doesn't request. These men opened up last time after months of
knowing you; disclosure followed trust, not a better question. The public
thread does the asking on day 3, and another dad Echoing someone by name on
day 5 is the win. See `eleven-honest-dads.html` for the full week.
