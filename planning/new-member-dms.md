# New member DMs — five days, Skool

Paste-ready. No markdown in the bodies: Skool DMs don't render it. No em dashes.

Send by hand. Skool flags bulk messaging, and a hand-sent DM is the reason the
last round came back 11 for 11.

## Sending load, honestly

Five messages per new dad. At ten new dads a week that is fifty DMs. It stays
survivable because only three of the five need anything personal:

  Day 1   copy-paste, unchanged every time
  Day 2   copy-paste, swap the thread link and the name
  Day 3   copy-paste, unchanged every time
  Day 4   copy-paste, swap the thread link
  Day 5   the only one that needs real writing

So the true cost is one thoughtful message per dad, plus four pastes.

Keep a list of who has had which day. That list is also your day 5 check.

---

## DAY 1 — the day he joins

Gives him something. Asks for nothing.

```
Greetings Captain,

James here. Glad you're in.

No questions, no homework. Just something I want you to have.

It's in the Classroom, under T.H.I.S. Captain's Log:

https://www.skool.com/dad-up-dojo-1685/classroom/9f8310e9?md=eb11a4fba15d4ac883056debd65254ff

Three things you're grateful for, plus an Echo. Ninety seconds. Print it or keep a notebook next to it, doesn't matter.

Read this bit before you start. You are not bound by calendar days. Miss a day, miss a month. There is no streak to break and nothing to feel behind on.

Nothing to reply to. No dad walks alone.

James
```

## DAY 2 — point at one thing

He does not need the whole community. He needs one door. Swap in a real
thread and a real name.

```
Quick one.

If you only open one thing in here this week, make it this:

[thread link]

[Name] put something real in there and the replies are worth five minutes.

Nothing to reply to. I just didn't want you to have to go looking.

James
```

---

## DAY 3 — teach the Echo

The move nobody arrives knowing. Still no ask.

```
One thing about the log that most guys skip.

The Echo is the part that does the work.

You go back to an earlier entry and ask two questions. Why did that happen. And what did it do for the people around me.

The first one is where you notice your own hand in it. The second one turns it outward.

That's the whole thing. Still nothing to reply to.

James
```

---

## DAY 4 — the first ask, and it is tiny

```
There's a thread running in the community. One line from your log this week.

One line. That's the whole ask, and plenty of them aren't deep.

[thread link]

If you're not ready, that's fine. It'll be there next week and the week after.

James
```

---

## DAY 5 — the only one you actually write

### If he posted

Reference the real thing he said. Never a generic well done.

```
Saw your line about [the actual thing]. Good one.

[one sentence responding to what he actually wrote]

That's the whole practice, by the way. You just did it.

Glad you're here.

James
```

### If he didn't post

Never "I noticed you haven't been active." That sentence makes a man feel
behind, and the whole promise is the opposite.

```
No agenda here. Just making sure you got everything you needed.

If the log is sitting unopened, that is genuinely fine. It'll keep.

And if there's something you'd want in here that isn't, tell me. I'd rather know.

James
```

---

## The rules these are built on

Day 1 gives and asks nothing. Your eleven opened up after months of knowing
you, not because of a good question. Trust came first.

Nothing is asked of him until day 4, and the ask is one line.

Day 5 is the only message that mentions him. Everything before it is about
the log or the room.

No message ever refers to how long he has been a member, what he has not done,
or how active he is.

The public thread does the heavy lifting. One post serves every new dad at once.
Make sure it exists and is warm before any of these go out.
